## Feature set

- Create an achievement with title, description, icon and rarity.
- Unlock an achievement from the dashboard to display it on your desktop for the rest of your day (until 00:00).
- Every time you unlock an achievement, you gain permanent XP based on its rarity. Fill the XP bar to level up.
- Earn a rank based on your average daily XP over the past 5 days.
- Browse your streak and past achievements in the 'Streaks & history' page.
- Connect to the dashboard to unlock achievements from your phone on the same local network.
